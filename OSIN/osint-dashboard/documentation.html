Guide to using the OSINT Intelligence Platform

Welcome to the Platform

This platform provides a comprehensive suite of tools for Open-Source Intelligence gathering, organization, and reporting. Designed for analysts, researchers, and educational purposes.

Quick Start

  1. Launch the platform
  2. Create a new investigation in the Investigations tab
  3. Select investigation modules from the Tools tab
  4. Collect and organize evidence
  5. Generate reports in the Reports tab

System Requirements

A modern web browser with JavaScript enabled (Chrome 90+, Firefox 90+, Edge 90+, Safari 15+). No backend or installation is required, all data is processed locally.

Investigation Guide

How to create an investigation

Go to the Investigations page and click "New Investigation". Fill out the required details such as Name, Targets, and Priority.

How to add evidence

Evidence can be added directly within an active investigation. Link sources, classify confidence, and attach descriptions.

How to add findings

Synthesize evidence into actionable findings. Assign severity levels from Low to Critical.

How to use the timeline

The timeline automatically updates with milestones, added evidence, and confirmed findings for a chronological view of events.

How to generate reports

Navigate to the Reports page, select your investigation, choose desired sections, and click Generate.

Best practices for OSINT research

Always verify information across multiple independent sources. Maintain objective and professional language in analyst notes.

Available Modules

  • 🌐 Websites & Search Engines: Basic domain lookups and search engine dorking capabilities.
  • 📱 Social Media & Public Profiles: Cross-platform username correlation and profile verification.
  • 📰 News & Public Records: Search aggregated news sources and open corporate registries.
  • 🗺️ Maps & Geolocation: Analyze location data and coordinate mapping.
  • 📧 Email & Domain Intelligence: Email validation, MX records, and domain infrastructure.
  • 🔎 Username & Domain Investigation: Deep dive into specific identity footprints.
  • 🖼️ Image & Video Intelligence: Reverse image search principles and metadata extraction.
  • 🔐 Cybersecurity Threat Intelligence: Correlation with known IOCs, CVEs, and threat feeds.
  • 🧩 Intelligence Correlation: Visual graph interface for mapping relationships between entities.

Security & Privacy

What the platform focuses on:

  • ✅ Authorized research
  • ✅ Educational purposes
  • ✅ Cybersecurity analysis
  • ✅ Publicly available information

What the platform does NOT do:

  • ❌ Harvest credentials
  • ❌ Bypass authentication
  • ❌ Access private accounts
  • ❌ Deploy malware
  • ❌ Perform unauthorized surveillance

Data Storage

All data is stored in the browser's localStorage. No data is sent to external servers. It is the User's responsibility to manage data retention and comply with regulations.
